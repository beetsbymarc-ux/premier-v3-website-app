The Story

Two Decades of
Sacramento Roots

Helen Wong began her real estate career in 2008 — right in the heart of the financial crisis. While others fled the market, Helen doubled down on Sacramento, learning every neighborhood, every price fluctuation, and every opportunity the city offered.

That baptism by fire forged something rare: a broker who truly understands the full market cycle, not just the good times. Helen has guided her clients through boom markets and corrections alike, always putting their long-term interests first.

Today, Premier Realty stands as one of Sacramento's most respected boutique real estate firms — small enough to provide white-glove personal service, experienced enough to compete with any agency in the market.
