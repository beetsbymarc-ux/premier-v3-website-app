The Journey

Sixteen Years of
Sacramento Roots

From starting her career in the teeth of the 2008 financial crisis to becoming one of Sacramento's most trusted Brokers — a journey built one client at a time.

2008

A Difficult Start

Helen earned her California real estate license just as the market was collapsing. While most new agents fled the industry, she learned the fundamentals under the hardest possible conditions — an education in market cycles no classroom could offer.

2012

Adding Mortgage Expertise

Helen earned her NMLS mortgage license (#281359), becoming a dual-licensed broker. This gave her a rare ability to guide clients through both the property search and the financing — eliminating the hand-offs that slow most transactions.

2016

Joining Premier Realty

Helen joined Premier Realty at 4030 Truxel Rd in Sacramento — a boutique firm focused on personalized, relationship-driven service rather than volume at all costs. A natural fit for the way she had always worked.

2020–2024

The Referral Years

As the market climbed, corrected, and climbed again, Helen's practice became almost entirely referral-driven. Families returned for second homes; first-time buyers sent their siblings, coworkers, and parents. The name on the sign became the name they trusted.

Today

850+ Families Later

Today, Helen guides her clients with the same patience and directness that has defined her career — a broker who answers her phone, explains every document, and still remembers the neighborhoods she sold in 2009.

How We Work

Our Philosophy

Real estate should feel like a partnership, not a transaction. Every decision is guided by three principles that have stayed unchanged since 2008.

Honest Counsel

Helen tells clients what they need to hear, not what they want to hear. Honest market data, realistic pricing, and straight talk about every offer — always.

Results Over Noise

No gimmicks, no open-house theatre. Every strategy is evaluated on one question: does this get our client the best outcome at the lowest risk?

People Over Paperwork

Behind every transaction is a family, a move, a life chapter. Helen treats each client the way she'd want her own family treated — and returns every call herself.
