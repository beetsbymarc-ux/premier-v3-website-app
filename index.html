Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about real estate in Sacramento

How long does it take to buy a home in Sacramento? ▼
The typical home-buying process in Sacramento takes 30–60 days from offer acceptance to closing. This timeline includes inspections, appraisal, loan approval, and final walkthrough. Helen can guide you through each phase and help expedite the process where possible.
What are the closing costs when selling a home? ▼
In California, sellers typically pay 5–6% of the sale price in closing costs, including real estate agent commissions, title insurance, and transfer taxes. Helen will provide a detailed breakdown of all costs before you list and help you understand your net proceeds.
Does Helen serve areas outside Sacramento? ▼
Helen primarily serves the Greater Sacramento region, including Natomas, Elk Grove, Land Park, East Sacramento, Midtown, Arden-Arcade, Roseville, Rocklin, West Sacramento, and surrounding areas. If you're looking outside these regions, she can refer you to trusted colleagues.
What languages does Helen speak? ▼
Helen is fluent in English and Spanish (Español). This bilingual expertise is particularly valuable for Sacramento's diverse community and ensures clear communication throughout every transaction.
How can I get a free home valuation? ▼
Simply fill out the contact form at the top of this page or call Helen directly at (916) 256-7073. She'll schedule a convenient time to discuss your property, current market conditions, and provide a free, no-obligation home valuation.
