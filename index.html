Where We Work

Serving Sacramento
& Beyond

Deep knowledge of every pocket of greater Sacramento — from tree-lined streets in East Sacramento to the waterfront communities along the river.

East Sacramento
Core Area · Premier Market
Craftsman bungalows, tree-canopied streets, and some of the city's most prestigious zip codes. Helen's specialty.
Land Park
Core Area · Family Favorite
Historic Tudor homes, William Land Park, and top-rated schools. A perennial favorite for families and investors.
Midtown
Core Area · Urban Lifestyle
Sacramento's cultural heartbeat — restaurants, galleries, and walkable energy that attracts professionals and creatives alike.
Natomas
Core Area · Growth Corridor
Growing community near the Sacramento Kings arena. Excellent value and investment potential in the city's north.
Elk Grove
Extended Service Area
Top-ranked schools, master-planned communities, and strong rental demand make Elk Grove a standout for families and investors.
Arden-Arcade
Extended Service Area
Mid-century homes, country club living, and convenient access to downtown. Highly walkable and community-driven.
Folsom
Extended Service Area
Luxury subdivisions, lake access, and tech-sector employers. Among Sacramento's most desirable suburban markets.
Rancho Cordova
Extended Service Area
Affordable entry points and strong rental yields. Ideal for first-time buyers and investment portfolio expansion.

Also serving Roseville, Rocklin, West Sacramento, Curtis Park, Oak Park, Citrus Heights, and surrounding communities.
